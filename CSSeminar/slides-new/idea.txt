Comprehensive Documentation for LWE Key Recovery Attack

This document details the entire process of the Learning With Errors (LWE) key recovery attack, incorporating brute force for cruel bits, probabilistic enumeration for the sloping region, and iterative recovery for cool bits. The methodology is designed to achieve efficiency by focusing on statistical properties of the secret and leveraging iterative refinements.

2. Variables and Regions

N: Total number of bits in the secret vector s

n_cruel: Number of cruel bits—these are hard to recover due to high variance and minimal reduction.

n_sloping: Number of sloping bits—these are intermediate in difficulty with variance between the cruel and cool thresholds.

n_cool = N - n_cruel - n_sloping: Number of cool bits—these are highly reduced and easier to recover.

hw(): Hamming weight of the secret, i.e., the total number of "1" bits in s

cruel_1(): Number of "1" bits recovered in the cruel region.

k = hw() - cruel_1(): Remaining "1" bits to be distributed across the sloping and cool regions.
